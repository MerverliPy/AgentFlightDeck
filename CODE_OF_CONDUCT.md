# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to maintaining a respectful, constructive, and inclusive project environment.

Participants are expected to engage professionally and focus on improving AgentFlightDeck.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of expected behavior include:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Giving constructive feedback
- Keeping discussions focused on the project
- Avoiding personal attacks
- Being clear when making technical claims
- Distinguishing between implemented features and planned ideas

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal insults
- Trolling or disruptive comments
- Repeated off-topic discussion
- Misrepresenting the project’s current maturity
- Publishing misleading claims about project capabilities

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Repeated or severe violations may result in restricted participation.
